# NNNN. Title as a short decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Scope:** packages or areas affected

## Context

What situation forces a decision? Include the facts that matter: constraints,
measurements, who is affected.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly. Include enough detail that someone could check
whether code follows it.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what we have to watch for. Include any
breaking changes.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we rejected and why, briefly.
